Editorial Policy

How we source, fact-check, and disclose — so you don't have to take any of it on faith.

Sourcing standards

We prioritize primary and authoritative sources over secondary commentary: government agencies and data (the IRS, the SEC, the Federal Reserve, the Bureau of Labor Statistics, the Social Security Administration, HUD, the Census Bureau), companies' own filings and official statements, and established, reputable financial and wire-service journalism when a primary source isn't directly available. Every figure cited in an article is sourced, and every article carries a visible "Sources" section at the bottom linking to exactly where that information came from.

Fact-checking

Each article is researched against current, dated sources before publication. Where a figure or rule (a contribution limit, a tax threshold, a market statistic) is time-sensitive, we say so and encourage readers to verify the current number before acting on it — these figures change, sometimes annually, and an article's publish date is not a guarantee that every number in it is still current by the time you read it.

Corrections

When we get something wrong, we fix it and say so. Substantive corrections — a wrong figure, a misattributed quote, a factual error that changes the meaning of an article — are noted visibly in the corrected article with the date of the correction. Minor copyediting fixes (typos, formatting) are not individually logged. Our full process is on the Corrections page.

Disclosure and editorial independence

Coverage decisions, conclusions, and story selection on this site are not influenced by advertisers, sponsors, or any commercial relationship. If a conflict of interest between our publisher and a company or project we cover were ever to exist, we'd disclose it explicitly at the top of that specific article — we wouldn't rely on a blanket footer notice to cover it. We don't accept payment in exchange for favorable coverage of any company, token, or product.

Not financial, tax, or legal advice

Everything on this site is informational and educational. Nothing here is a personalized recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any investment, and nothing here should replace advice from a licensed financial advisor, CPA, or attorney who knows your specific situation.

Questions about a specific article's sourcing or a potential conflict of interest we may have missed? See our Contact page.
