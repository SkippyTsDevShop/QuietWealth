Corrections

We get things wrong sometimes. Here's how we handle it when we do.

How to report an error

If you spot a factual error — a wrong figure, an outdated number, a misattributed quote, a broken source link — email us at contact@quietwealth.news with "Correction" in the subject line and a link to the article. We read every report.

What we do when we find or confirm an error

  • Substantive errors — a wrong figure, a misattributed quote, or anything that changes the meaning or accuracy of an article — are corrected in the article itself, with a visible note stating what was changed and the date of the correction.
  • Minor errors — typos, formatting issues, broken links that don't affect the article's factual content — are fixed without an individual log entry.
  • We don't silently rewrite substantive claims. If a figure or conclusion changes materially after publication, readers should be able to see that it changed, not just find a quietly different number.

Corrections log

No corrections have been logged yet. When one is issued, it will be noted here along with a link to the corrected article.
