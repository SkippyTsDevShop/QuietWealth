Privacy Policy

A short policy for a small site: here's what we actually collect, and what we don't.

What we collect

  • Newsletter subscriptions. If you subscribe to our newsletter, your email address is collected and stored by our newsletter provider, Beehiiv, to send you our weekly email. We don't separately store or resell subscriber emails ourselves.
  • Standard web server and analytics data. Like most websites, our hosting and analytics tools may log basic technical information automatically — things like IP address, browser type, and pages visited — used only in aggregate to understand traffic and site performance.
  • Nothing else. We don't have user accounts, we don't collect financial account information, and we don't ask visitors to submit personal financial details anywhere on this site.

How newsletter data is handled

Our newsletter runs on Beehiiv, a third-party email platform. When you subscribe, your email address is stored and processed under Beehiiv's own privacy policy, which governs how that data is secured and used. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any newsletter email, which removes your address from our sending list.

Cookies and tracking

This site may use basic analytics cookies to understand aggregate traffic patterns. We don't use this information to build individual advertising profiles, and we don't sell visitor data to third parties.

Children's privacy

QuietWealth.news is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our tools or practices change. The version posted here is always the current one.

Questions

Reach us at Contact with any privacy-related questions.

This is a general privacy policy for a small independent publisher and is not an exhaustive legal document. It does not constitute legal advice.
